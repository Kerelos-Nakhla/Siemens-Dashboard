## 📌 Executive Overview

The **HR Attrition & Workforce Risk Intelligence System** is an enterprise people analytics solution built in Power BI. Engineered for Chief Human Resources Officers (CHROs), talent acquisition leaders, and department heads, the system analyzes employee attrition patterns across **1,470 workforce records**, isolating the root causes of voluntary turnover, compensation friction, burnout indicators, and career progression stagnation.

Featuring a modern dark-mode executive interface rendered through dynamic HTML/CSS visual containers and native Power BI visuals, the dashboard transforms raw HR data into strategic talent retention intelligence across **6 specialized analytical views**.

## 🔍 Critical Flight Risk Catalysts (Root-Cause Discovery)

### 1. Overtime Burnout Multiplier
- Employees working regular **Overtime** exhibit an alarming **30.53% attrition rate** (127 departures out of 416 employees).
- In contrast, employees **without overtime** have an attrition rate of only **10.44%** (110 departures out of 1,054 employees).
- **Overtime introduces a +20.09% net flight risk penalty**, making sustained extra hours the primary operational hazard for voluntary departures.

### 2. Compensation Compression & Salary Brackets
| Monthly Salary Group | Headcount | Departures | Attrition Rate % | Risk Profile |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Under $3,000 (< 3K)** | 395 | 113 | **28.61%** | 🚨 Entry-level wage vulnerability |
| **$8,000 – $12,000 (8–12K)** | 186 | 29 | **15.59%** | 🟡 Senior individual contributor plateau |
| **$3,000 – $5,000 (3–5K)** | 354 | 50 | **14.12%** | 🟡 Mid-tier market competition |
| **$5,000 – $8,000 (5–8K)** | 340 | 34 | **10.00%** | 🟢 Competitive professional compensation |
| **Above $12,000 (+12K)** | 195 | 11 | **5.64%** | 🟢 Executive retention golden handcuffs |

### 3. Early Career & Generation Cohorts
| Age Group | Total Employees | Departures | Attrition Rate % | Strategic Insight |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **18–25 Years** | 123 | 44 | **35.77%** | Extreme early-tenure flight risk; high onboarding cost |
| **26–35 Years** | 606 | 116 | **19.14%** | Primary talent builder core; career mobility pressure |
| **56+ Years** | 47 | 8 | **17.02%** | Pre-retirement transitions |
| **46–55 Years** | 226 | 26 | **11.50%** | Established senior tenure |
| **36–45 Years** | 468 | 43 | **9.19%** | Most stable career lifecycle phase |

### 4. Business Travel Stress
- **Travel Frequently:** **24.91% attrition** (69 left out of 277).
- **Travel Rarely:** **14.96% attrition** (156 left out of 1,043).
- **Non-Travel:** **8.00% attrition** (12 left out of 150).
- Frequent travel triples employee departure likelihood compared to non-traveling peers.

### 5. Marital & Demographic Disparities
- **Single Employees:** **25.53% attrition** (120 departures out of 470) — significantly higher mobility than **Married** (12.48%) and **Divorced** (10.09%) personnel.
- **Gender Balance:** Males experience **17.01% attrition** ($6,380.51 avg salary) versus Females at **14.80% attrition** ($6,686.57 avg salary).

## 📐 Core DAX Measures & Formulas Reference

All business calculations are centralized within the `DAX Measures` semantic table, paired with dynamic UI renderers in `HTML Visuals`:

### 1. People Analytics Core Measures (`DAX Measures`)

```dax
// --- Census & Headcount Aggregations ---
total_employees = COUNTROWS(fact_attrition)

total_left_employees = 
CALCULATE(
    [total_employees],
    fact_attrition[Employee Status] = "Left"
)

total_stayed_employees = 
CALCULATE(
    [total_employees],
    fact_attrition[Employee Status] = "Stay"
)

// --- Attrition & Stability Ratios ---
rate_left_employees = 
DIVIDE(
    [total_left_employees],
    [total_employees]
)

rate_stayed_employees = 
DIVIDE(
    [total_stayed_employees],
    [total_employees]
)

// --- Compensation & Tenure Averages ---
average_monthly_income = AVERAGE(fact_attrition[Monthly Income])

average_tenure = AVERAGE(fact_attrition[Total Working Years])

average_age = AVERAGE(dim_employee[Age])

// --- Root Cause & Risk Diagnostics ---
attrition_rate_over_time_difference = 
VAR Rate_Yes = CALCULATE([rate_left_employees], fact_attrition[Over Time] = "Yes")
VAR Rate_No = CALCULATE([rate_left_employees], fact_attrition[Over Time] = "No")
RETURN Rate_Yes - Rate_No

highest_attrition_age_group = 
VAR Age_Groups = VALUES(dim_employee[Group_Age])
VAR Rate_By_Age = ADDCOLUMNS(Age_Groups, "Attrition_Rate", CALCULATE([rate_left_employees]))
VAR Top_Group = TOPN(1, Rate_By_Age, [Attrition_Rate], DESC)
VAR First_Group_Name = MAXX(Top_Group, dim_employee[Group_Age])
VAR First_Group_Rate = MAXX(Top_Group, [Attrition_Rate])
RETURN First_Group_Name & " - " & FORMAT(First_Group_Rate, "0.0%")

highes_risk_tenure_group = 
VAR Tenure_Group = VALUES(fact_attrition[Group_Years_At_Company])
VAR Tenure_Group_And_Rate = ADDCOLUMNS(Tenure_Group, "Attrition Rate", [rate_left_employees])
VAR First_Group_Name_And_Rate = TOPN(1, Tenure_Group_And_Rate, [Attrition Rate], DESC)
VAR First_Group_Name = MAXX(First_Group_Name_And_Rate, fact_attrition[Group_Years_At_Company])
VAR First_Group_Rate = MAXX(First_Group_Name_And_Rate, [Attrition Rate])
RETURN First_Group_Name & " - " & FORMAT(First_Group_Rate, "0.0%")
```

## 🏗️ Data Architecture & Star Schema

The data model connects normalized human resources dimensions to transactional employee status records:

```
                  ┌────────────────────────┐
                  │      dim_employee      │
                  │ (Age, Gender, Marital) │
                  └───────────┬────────────┘
                              │ 1
                              │
                              │ *
┌──────────────────┐   ┌──────┴───────────┐   ┌──────────────────┐
│  dim_department  │──<│  fact_attrition  │>──│     dim_job      │
│ (Dept ID, Name)  │   │  (1,470 records) │   │ (Role, Level)    │
└──────────────────┘   └──────┬───────────┘   └──────────────────┘
                              │ *
                              │
                              │ 1
                  ┌───────────┴────────────┐
                  │  dim_business_travel   │
                  │  dim_education         │
                  └────────────────────────┘
```

- **Fact Table:**
  - `fact_attrition` (1,470 records, 30 columns): Central workforce census table containing monthly income, working years, overtime status, satisfaction scores (environment, relationship, job involvement, work-life balance), performance ratings, and departure records.
- **Dimension Tables:**
  - `dim_employee` (1,470 records): Employee demographic profile (Age, Gender, Marital Status, Age Bracket).
  - `dim_department` (3 records): Operating business divisions (Sales, R&D, Human Resources).
  - `dim_job` (26 records): Job title classifications, roles, and job seniority tiers.
  - `dim_education` (30 records): Academic degrees and educational discipline fields.
  - `dim_business_travel` (3 records): Travel requirements (Non-Travel, Travel Rarely, Travel Frequently).
- **Specialized Table:**
  - `HTML Visuals`: Dynamic SVG and HTML measure library delivering interactive executive layouts directly in Power BI.

## ⚙️ ETL & Power Query Pipeline

1. **Extraction & Transformation:**
   - Raw employee attributes unpivoted and split into modular dimensional entities using Power Query (M).
   - Numeric tenure, age, and compensation variables discretized into logical reporting tiers (`Group_Age`, `Group_Salary`, `Group_Years_At_Company`).
2. **Data Cleansing & Validation:**
   - Attrition flags (`Yes`/`No`) transformed into standardized explicit status dimensions (`Left` / `Stay`).
   - Currency fields validated as `Fixed Decimal Number` / `Currency` to prevent calculation distortion.
3. **Satisfaction Index Consolidation:**
   - Composite satisfaction indicators normalized across Environment, Job, Relationship, and Work-Life balance dimensions.
